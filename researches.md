## Research 001: Automated Feature Engineering in ML Pipelines

**Status:** In Progress

**Focus:** Automated feature selection and representation learning for structured/tabular data

**Problem:** Feature engineering remains a critical bottleneck in ML workflows. This research investigates methods for automatically discovering optimal feature representations that generalize across model families.

**Datasets:**
- Abalone (regression task)

**Key Topics:**
- Automated feature selection
- Feature importance metrics (model-based, statistical)
- Dimensionality reduction
- Cross-model feature robustness
- Handling multicollinearity and feature interactions
